# CampusOS building models

Owner authorized integration on 2026-11-01 and corrected visibility/rendering on 2026-11-02. Youyuan and Shiyuan dormitory GLBs were added on 2026-11-12. Youyuan 2.5D is the same plain white mass style as the North/South dormitories; its existing 3D GLB SHA256 is preserved. Shiyuan 1–4 are independent entries with exact source outlines; six stories/18 metres and facade details are visual estimates. No reference photographs, image textures, supplier meshes or external buffer resources are published. Footprints © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL-1.0. Source/approval/hash/origins are recorded in src/features/campus-map/data/building-model-catalog.json and docs/model-previews.

Metric heights and photo-informed courtyard/facade details remain visual approximations, not surveyed GIS or indoor/navigation facts. Original campus map and walking network are unchanged. South-7 keeps its approved public V2 3D file; its unchanged 21-metre footprint/courtyard uses the shared 2.5D material/light path with its original native volume as a failure fallback. Completed 3D models remain detailed across zoom and pan. A-D uses V2 with the corrected B clock tower. Shared-origin components are positioned in their GLBs.
