# CampusOS building models

Owner authorized integration on 2026-11-01 and corrected visibility/rendering on 2026-11-02. Youyuan, Shiyuan 1–4, the Student Activity Center and the Academic Exchange Center GLBs were added on 2026-11-12/13. Youyuan 2.5D is the same plain white mass style as the North/South dormitories; its existing 3D GLB SHA256 is preserved. Shiyuan 1–4 are independent entries with exact source outlines; six stories/18 metres and facade details are visual estimates. The Student Activity Center remains one GIS entity; its white 2.5D volume and north-up corrected blue hall/red tower plus display-only central opening are visual estimates. The Academic Exchange Center keeps its source relation outer ring and two courtyard rings; grey hip roofs, four levels and facade details follow the administration/faculty visual reference and remain estimates. The Wencai Garden (First Cafeteria) model was added on 2026-11-13: a plain white 2.5D footprint volume and a southwest-facing 3D colonnade with orange upper storey, stairs, two-level display bridge and schematic solar roof. Three levels and all metric heights are photo estimates; no new routes or entrances are asserted. Boya Garden (Second Cafeteria) was added on 2026-11-13 with owner-confirmed four levels, a north-pointing triangular mass, the southern orange screen parallel to Shiyuan, a stepped south annex, exposed white stairs and roof frames. All metric heights and detailed roof/facade subdivisions are visual estimates. Its 2.5D asset is a plain white source volume. Fengcai Garden (Third Cafeteria) was added on 2026-11-13 with owner-confirmed three levels, a plain white 2.5D source volume and an east-facing 3D glazed entrance, white portico columns, silver stair tower at its south end, western curved roof rim and roof service strip. All metric heights and roof/facade subdivisions remain visual estimates. No reference photographs, image textures, supplier meshes or external buffer resources are published. Footprints © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL-1.0. Source/approval/hash/origins are recorded in src/features/campus-map/data/building-model-catalog.json and docs/model-previews.

Metric heights and photo-informed courtyard/facade details remain visual approximations, not surveyed GIS or indoor/navigation facts. Original campus map and walking network are unchanged. South-7 keeps its approved public V2 3D file; its unchanged 21-metre footprint/courtyard uses the shared 2.5D material/light path with its original native volume as a failure fallback. Completed 3D models remain detailed across zoom and pan. A-D uses V2 with the corrected B clock tower. Shared-origin components are positioned in their GLBs.
